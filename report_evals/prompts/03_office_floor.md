# Eval 03: Office Floor

## Prompt

Design a small office floor with the following program:
- Overall dimensions: 20m x 12m
- Reception area near the entrance (approximately 4m x 4m)
- 3 private offices along the north wall (each 4m x 3m)
- Open workspace in the remaining area
- A corridor connecting all spaces

Requirements:
- Main entrance on the south wall (double door, 1.8m wide)
- Each private office has a single door to the corridor
- Large windows on the east and west exterior walls
- Use wall with glazing on the south facade

Generate:
- IFC model
- DXF drawings (floor plan, elevations, section)
- PDF drawing set

## Difficulty

Hard

## Skills Tested

- Complex floor plan layout
- Multiple room types
- Corridor circulation logic
- Door scheduling (multiple doors with marks)
- Window placement patterns

## Design Constraints

- Doors and windows must have at least 300mm clearance from wall corners/ends
- Doors and windows must have at least 300mm clearance from each other
- Interior walls must not connect to exterior walls at door/window locations
