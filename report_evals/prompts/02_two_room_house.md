# Eval 02: Two Room House

## Prompt

Create a small house with two rooms: a living room (6m x 5m) and a bedroom (4m x 5m). The rooms should share a wall. The living room should have a large window (2m wide) on the south wall and an entry door. The bedroom should have a medium window on the north wall. Use exterior walls with brick finish (100mm) over concrete structure (150mm). Add an interior door connecting the two rooms.

Generate:
- IFC model
- DXF drawings (floor plan, elevations, section)
- PDF drawing set

## Difficulty

Medium

## Skills Tested

- Multiple rooms with shared walls
- Compound wall types with layers
- Interior vs exterior wall differentiation
- Multiple door types (entry vs interior)
- Wall join handling

## Design Constraints

- Doors and windows must have at least 300mm clearance from wall corners/ends
- Doors and windows must have at least 300mm clearance from each other
- Interior walls must not connect to exterior walls at door/window locations
